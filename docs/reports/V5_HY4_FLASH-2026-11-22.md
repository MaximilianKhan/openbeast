## 1. Executive summary

- **Hy4-flash (NVFP4) passed 60/71 (SCORE 83.4) under arm H_T2.** H_T2 is
  DeepSeek's T2 exactly: the composite harness, an 8,192-token per-turn
  thinking cap and the act nudge. It is #2 on the v5 board, behind
  DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash (Mia EXL3) T2 (65/71, 94.9) and ahead of
  GLM-5.3-Flash C (51/71 scored, 76.7). The Claude Opus 5.5 reference
  (71/71, a different scaffold) stays unranked.
- **Against DeepSeek on the same harness and pair: 5 units behind, single
  seed.** The two rows ran on the same harness9 tree (commit f38a29c7, era
  036638dd), on the same two DGX Sparks, from the same Mac harness host.
  Hy4 lost 7 units DeepSeek passed and gained 2. By the phrasing rules, a
  gap of 3 or more on the same harness reads "behind, single seed". The
  measured noise on this suite is about ±1 unit (DeepSeek T vs T_s1).
- **It is a model-and-weights comparison, not a model comparison.** Hy4
  runs atakans' NVFP4 build on vLLM; DeepSeek runs Mia's 2.9 bpw EXL3 on
  TensorFold. The quantization, the engine and the drafter all differ.
- **Strong in the compiled languages, behind in Python.** C++ 8/8 and Rust
  8/8 (DeepSeek 7/8 and 8/8); Python 26/32 against DeepSeek's 30/32.
  LANG 89.0 against DeepSeek's 88.9: when Hy4 solves a task, it ports it
  about as well. The gap is in SOLVE (81.5 against 96.9).
- **It works more turns with less thinking per turn.** Mean 29.6 agent
  turns per unit against DeepSeek's 19.2, and 22.6K completion tokens per
  unit against 37.1K. It hit the wall on 5 units (2 passed at the wall)
  against DeepSeek's 13. Its prompt tokens are higher (47.9M against
  28.1M), because more turns re-send the context more often: 825K tokens
  per pass against 473K.
- **The ablation, H_C (GLM's best configuration on Hy4): 55/71, SCORE
  82.2.** Same harness9 tree, served model and pair as H_T2. T2's setup
  passed 5 more (7 gained, 2 lost): **helped, single seed.** The SCORE gap
  is only 1.2, because all 7 of T2's gains are ports (non-Python variants:
  LANG 89.0 against 74.8) while H_C's SOLVE is a little higher (84.6
  against 81.5). It is a **bundled** change: H_T2 against H_C switches the
  assists, the 8K per-turn cap and the act nudge together, so the +5
  cannot be attributed to any one of them (§5).
- **Across the three models** (§5.3): on Hy4 the T2 bundle helped by 5;
  on DeepSeek the cap alone helped in three same-harness pairs on
  harness10 (U → U_8k +4, UP → UP_8k +3, UPA → UPA_8k +3); on GLM the
  assists, switched on together with a harness change (arm E against C),
  left it level (−1, unresolved: no seed repeat).
- **Integrity scan: 0 contaminated** in both arms (H_T2 71 transcripts,
  H_C 71; 71 units each). 0 units lost to infrastructure.
- **DeepSeek stays the pair's default and T2 its setup.** Hy4 is staged:
  `beasthy4` serves it by hand, never at boot.

### 5.2 Units gained and lost

H_C → H_T2 over 71 units (the generated table in §4): **net +5, 7 gained
and 2 lost. By the phrasing rules (3 or more on the same harness):
helped, single seed.**

- Gained by T2's setup (7): 122_gemm_blocked_d, 122_gemm_blocked_e,
  158_karatsuba_bytes_d, 163_bigint_divmod_b, 163_bigint_divmod_c,
  163_bigint_divmod_f, 168_linearizability_kv_e. All seven are ports
  (Go, C, C++, Rust, Zig variants), none a Python base: hence LANG 89.0
  against 74.8. In H_C, three of the seven hit the wall (one after only 4
  turns, one after 22, one after 32) and four ran to the 60-turn ceiling;
  under T2 they passed in 11 to 60 turns.
- Lost by T2's setup (2): 122_gemm_blocked_a (Go) and 124_rkf45 (Python).
  H_C passed both; with 124 that is one more Python base, so H_C's SOLVE
  is a little higher.
- Per language (§4): Zig 4/6 against 2/6, Rust 8/8 against 6/8, C++ 8/8
  against 7/8, C 7/8 against 6/8; Python 26/32 against 27/32; Go and Sh
  equal.

**The wall.** H_C hit the wall on 13 units (4 passed) against H_T2's 5
(2 passed). H_C's wall-hit units had 4 to 60 turns, three of them under
12 (4, 9 and 11): long thinking turns at the 16,384-token cap used the wall before the
agent acted. H_T2's wall hits all came after 49 or more turns. That is the
pattern DeepSeek showed at a 16K cap (U's losses ran out of wall at 11-26
turns, [the DeepSeek report](V5_DEEPSEEK_CAMPAIGN-2026-11-20.md)).

**Tokens.** H_C wrote 23% more completion tokens (1.97M against 1.61M;
27.7K a unit against 22.6K; about 1,080 a turn against 760) in fewer turns
(25.7 a unit against 29.6), and so re-sent less context: 35.1M prompt
tokens against 47.9M. Per pass, H_C is cheaper in total tokens (675K
against 825K) and dearer in completion tokens (35.8K against 26.8K).
Thirteen H_C rows and five H_T2 rows are wall-killed and counted to their
last reply (partial stats).

How to read it:

- **On Hy4 the change is bundled.** H_T2 against H_C switches the assists,
  the cap and the nudge at once. The +5 is T2's whole bundle; it does not
  say which switch earned it. The wall pattern (three H_C wall hits before
  12 turns, none in H_T2) and the completion tokens per turn point at the
  cap, as on DeepSeek, but that is a reading, not a measurement: the arm
  that would separate them (Hy4 with the assists and no cap, or the cap
  and no assists) has not run.
- **On DeepSeek the cap was measured alone** inside harness10, three
  times: U → U_8k +4, UP → UP_8k +3, UPA → UPA_8k +3. T → T2 (+4) also
  adds the act nudge and crosses harness8 → harness9.
- **On GLM the assists were switched on together with a harness change**
  (E against C, eras 8eaa5e5f → f1e0565c) and left it level (−1,
  unresolved: no seed repeat). GLM never ran the 8,192-token cap (its
  thinking arms ran the 16,384 eval turn cap). GLM's arms are of other
  harness eras (C 8eaa5e5f, E f1e0565c, against Hy4's 036638dd), so its
  row is context, not a same-harness comparison.
- **Together:** the per-turn cap is the one switch with a measured gain
  on its own (DeepSeek, three same-harness pairs); the assists showed no
  gain on GLM, in a cross-era pair. Hy4's +5 is consistent with that, and
  does not test it.

The next arm that would test it on Hy4 is H_T2 without the cap and nudge

## 6. Caveats

- **Single seed.** One run per arm. The suite's measured noise is about ±1
  unit; a 5-unit gap is outside it, but it is still one seed.
- **Thinking at reasoning_effort "high", not mapped.** The runner cannot
  send Hy4's `reasoning_effort` yet (an era change), so H_T2 thinks at the
  model's default. "max" was not measured; thinking off is not reachable.
- **NVFP4 against EXL3.** DeepSeek's row is a 2.9 bpw EXL3 pack on
  TensorFold, Hy4's an NVFP4 build on vLLM. The comparison is of model plus
  weights plus engine, on one harness and one pair.
- **The harness was tuned on DeepSeek.** T2 is DeepSeek's best measured
  setup; Hy4 runs it unchanged. A harness change observed on one model does
  not establish a result on another, which is what H_C is for.
- **The ablation is bundled.** H_C differs from H_T2 in the assists, the
  per-turn cap and the act nudge together; §5.3 says what that allows.
- **The speed probe is not the eval's workload.** It measured decode on
  512-token code prompts; the eval's turns carry long contexts.
