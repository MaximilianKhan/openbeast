# E23 — MoE expert-stack correction on Qwen3.6-35B-A3B (the centerpiece rung)

Date: 2026-08-04. Status: COMPLETE — decisive negative, both variants.
Companion: REPORT-capture.md (pooled expert-stack Gram capture, all gates
green). Protocol: methodology v2 (PROTOCOL.md) — compression stated against
the TARGETED reference.

## Hypothesis

1. Whitened low-rank residual correction extends from dense 2D tensors to 3D
   MoE expert stacks, using the POOLED per-stack activation Gram as the
   whitening metric (capture E23).
2. Cross-expert structure exists: a SINGLE shared output basis B per stack
   (from the pooled whitened residual) captures most of what 256 per-expert
   bases capture, at a fraction of the bytes — because all experts of a stack
   read/write the same activation spaces.

Falsifier for (2): shared-basis capture/KLD collapses relative to per-expert
at equal rank. Falsifier for the campaign question: neither variant beats the
same-size ladder rival with paired stats.

## Method

1. **Base**: requant to Q2_K with the gram35b imatrix and all three expert
   stacks regex-pinned to Q2_K (`--tensor-type "ffn_(up|gate|down)_exps=q2_k"`),
   preventing silent promotion. Achieved types verified from the log: all 120
   expert stacks q2_K; dense mix per default Q2_K recipe (attn q2_K/q3_K...,
   ffn_down_shexp q3_K, output q6_K).
2. **Extraction** (`e23_extract_moe.py`): per stack W (n_e=256, n_out, n_in),
   R_e = deq(ref_e) - deq(base_e); right-whitened Rd_e = R_e @ chol(G/count)
   with the POOLED stack Gram (gate maps to up's Gram — bitwise-verified
   shared input; count = routed-pair total, per REPORT-capture.md). The
   MoE-imatrix `counts.mean()` trap (review B10) is bypassed entirely: the
   extractor consumes only Grams.
   - Variant PE (per-expert): B_e = top-r left singular vectors of Rd_e
     (eigh on the small side), A_e = B_e^T R_e (projection form — the
     whitener cancels exactly, same optimality proof as 2D).
   - Variant SH (shared basis): B = top-r eigvecs of sum_e Rd_e Rd_e^T
     (= column-space SVD of the column-concatenated pooled whitened
     residual), A_e = B^T R_e — exact argmin of
     sum_e ||(R_e - B A_e) L||_F^2 over free A_e for orthonormal B.
   - r = 32 uniform, factors Q8_0, alpha = 32 (runtime scale 1).
3. **Serving**: llama.cpp's `build_lora_mm_id` already serves 3D pairs
   natively (nested `ggml_mul_mat_id` over lora_a/lora_b) — variant PE runs
   on STOCK llama.cpp. Export ne: lora_a (n_in, r, 256), lora_b (r, n_out,
   256); loader shape checks (`llama-adapter.cpp:362-367`) pass since they
   compare only ne[0]/ne[1]. Variant SH (2D lora_b shared across experts)
   needs a one-branch graph extension: in `build_lora_mm_id`, when
   `lw->b->ne[2]==1 && w->ne[2]>1`, apply B with a broadcast `ggml_mul_mat`
   instead of the second `ggml_mul_mat_id`. Patched in an isolated worktree
   (`.wt-e23`, HEAD 0ef6e55ed) — stock binary kept for every non-SH row.
4. **Eval**: PPL + KLD + same-top vs our reference logits, 20 chunks; paired
   per-chunk statistics on any margin under 2 sigma unpaired (per-chunk
   values recovered from the cumulative per-chunk log lines).

## Byte accounting (exact, from tensor dims; Q8_0 = 1.0625 B/param)

Expert stacks: 3 stacks/layer x 40 layers x 256 experts x (512x2048) =
32.212 B params (91% of the model).

| piece | params | bytes |
|---|---|---|
| PE adapter r32: per stack A_e 256x(32 x n_in) + B_e 256x(n_out x 32) | 2.517 B | 2.674 GB |
| SH adapter r32: per stack A_e 256x(32 x n_in) + one B (n_out x 32) | 1.514 B | 1.609 GB |

The x256 byte explosion of naive per-expert factors is real but asymmetric:
sharing B saves 20% on up/gate stacks (B_e is the thin side there) and 4x on
down stacks (B_e is the fat 2048x32 side). r=32 on a 512-min-dim expert is a
6.25% rank fraction; we kept r=32 for both variants (mission spec) rather
than equalizing bytes.

## Whitened-energy captured (r=32, offline capture metric)

| stack kind | per-expert (mean [min-max]) | shared-basis | random-basis floor r/n_out |
|---|---|---|---|
| gate (40) | 0.363 [0.311-0.517] | 0.090 [0.082-0.132] | 0.0625 |
| up (40) | 0.359 [0.308-0.507] | 0.086 [0.079-0.113] | 0.0625 |
| down (40) | 0.191 [0.150-0.522] | 0.022 [0.021-0.031] | 0.0156 |
| pooled total |  0.3772 | 0.0911 | - |

**The cross-expert hypothesis fails at the capture level**: one shared
output basis captures barely above what a random orthonormal basis would
(0.09 vs 0.0625 floor on up/gate; 0.022 vs 0.0156 on down). Precise
statement (reworded 2026-08-04 per adversarial-round2-experiments F9 —
256 subspaces cannot be literally mutually orthogonal in a 512-dim
space): the POOLED whitened residual spectrum is nearly isotropic —
shared capture ≈ the random floor — so there is no fat shared subspace
to exploit at r=32. Caveat on the per-expert numbers: captures
(0.36/0.19) are measured under the POOLED whitener, a disclosed
approximation of each expert's conditional input covariance (~256
samples/expert made per-expert Grams infeasible); some of the
"unstructured noise" reading may be whitener mismatch, mostly on down
stacks. Neither point touches the paired verdicts.

## Servability (measured, not assumed)

- PE (3D lora pairs, ne a=(n_in,r,256) b=(r,n_out,256)): **loads and runs on
  STOCK llama.cpp** (llama-perplexity exit 0, sane PPL) — `build_lora_mm_id`
  already nests `ggml_mul_mat_id` over both factors. No code change needed.
- SH (2D lora_b): stock binary **segfaults** (`ggml_mul_mat_id` asserts on a
  2D expert tensor; ids->ne[1]==b->ne[2] fails -> core dump, measured exit
  139). Served via a one-branch extension in `build_lora_mm_id`
  (worktree `.wt-e23`, llama-graph.cpp): when `lw->b->ne[2]==1 && w->ne[2]>1`
  apply B with a broadcast `ggml_mul_mat` after the per-expert
  `ggml_mul_mat_id` of A. Patched binary: exit 0.
- CPU note: on repacked CPU bufts the loras fall back to plain CPU buft
  (loader warning), as upstream intends.

## Verdict

**Both corrections are real; both LOSE the ladder — decisively.**

1. The machinery transfers to MoE: per-expert whitened correction improves
   the base at 4.6 sigma paired (18/20 chunks), served on stock llama.cpp
   with 3D lora pairs. Shared-basis improves at 2.7 sigma (13/20) — barely.
2. The campaign question — does either variant beat the same-size ladder
   rival with paired stats? **NO.** At its own byte point (14.5 GB) the PE
   adapter loses to the trivial interpolation control (promote down_exps to
   q4_K) by +0.0257 KLD at 6.9 sigma, 0/20 chunk wins; Q3_K_S at +0.7 GB is
   ~2x better again. Per byte spent, quant-type promotion buys ~4x more KLD
   reduction than rank-32 Q8 factors at this scale.
3. **Why (mechanism, from the capture numbers):** whitened-residual capture
   at r=32 is only 0.36 (up/gate) / 0.19 (down) per expert — versus 0.7+ in
   the dense 0.6B/0.8B experiments. Each expert is a small 512x2048 matrix
   whose Q2_K->Q4_K residual is nearly unstructured quantization noise; 256
   such matrices per stack make the correction pay the thin-factor overhead
   256 times while capturing flat spectra. And the cross-expert shared basis
   is only epsilon above the random-basis floor (0.09 vs 0.0625; 0.022 vs
   0.0156) — the pooled residual spectrum is nearly isotropic (see the
   reworded capture section), so there is no shared low-rank structure
   to amortize across experts.
4. What would change the verdict: (a) rank so low the bytes stop mattering
   (r<=8) — but capture would drop below ~0.15; (b) correcting the DENSE
   tensors of the MoE (attn/shexp, where our dense-scale wins live) instead
   of the expert stacks; (c) a base low enough (sub-2-bit exps) that the
   ladder has no cheap promotion rung left. All are follow-ups, not rescues.

Falsifier check (from the hypothesis section): (2) falsified exactly as
specified — shared-basis capture collapsed to the floor. The campaign
falsifier also triggered: neither variant beats the same-size rival.

## Caveats

- All rows share requant-of-Q4_K_M provenance (no BF16 exists on the rig);
  compression C is against the targeted Q4_K_M per protocol rule 0. KLD is
  against Q4_K_M logits, not BF16 — near-reference comparisons would carry
  the silent-zone caveat, but every verdict margin here is >=2.7 sigma
  paired and the losing margins are 6.9-8.2 sigma.
- Calibration wikitext-2-train (16-chunk Grams + imatrix), eval
  wikitext-2-test only; no held-out second corpus was run because no
  generality claim is being made from a negative.
- Speed/serving tax NOT measured (GPU fully contended by other agents;
  protocol rule 5 forbids non-same-session speed pairs). The dense-model
  serving tax (20-40% decode on stock adapters) presumably worsens with
  nested mul_mat_id; unmeasured.
- 16-chunk Grams are smoke-scale; a longer capture could lift SH capture
  slightly, but the gap to the floor is structural, not statistical.
