## A1. Robust statistics

All differences are A − B of per-chunk mean KL divergence (nats/token); negative favours A. `p(t)` is two-sided with df = n − 1. Sign test is the exact two-sided binomial on non-tied chunks. Bootstrap: 10,000 resamples, seed 20261009, percentile interval. Block bootstrap resamples non-overlapping contiguous blocks of 10 chunks; `t on block means` is a one-sample t on the block means (a check that does not assume independent chunks).
