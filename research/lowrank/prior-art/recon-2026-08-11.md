## THREATS (ranked by how much the paper must change)

### T1. GSQ — Gumbel-Softmax re-optimization of shipped GGUF K-quant checkpoints — CONFIRMED
**arXiv 2604.18556v2 (2026-05-15), IST-DASLab (Alistarh) + Red Hat AI + ETH; code: github.com/IST-DASLab/GSQ**
Verified from the PDF: "the same discrete-assignment optimization can
be applied to practical GGUF K-Quant checkpoints: starting from
publicly released GGUF models, GSQ improves accuracy while projecting
the result back into the same deployment format." Unsloth Qwen3-8B
Q3_K_M and Q2_K improved in-format; Q2_K avg (AIME25/GPQA-D/MMLU-Pro)
50.03 → 56.28. Scales to Kimi-K2.5 (trillion-param MoE).
**What it scoops:** gguf-refine's headline frame — "existing GGUF
K-quant artifacts are improvable in-format, stock serving." That
sentence is now published, by the strongest group in the field, with
public code.
**What survives:** GSQ is a gradient-trained method (learnable logits
per weight, Gumbel sampling, temperature annealing — a training loop
with optimizer state near model size); gguf-refine is one-shot,
backprop-free, imatrix-whitened error feedback. GSQ's objective is
layer reconstruction; ours is whitened, with paired-KLD proof at 0.6B
AND 27B. No evidence they re-optimize sub-block scales.
**Paper change (required):** reposition gguf-refine as *the cheap
deterministic point on the GSQ curve* — one pass, no gradients, no
sampling, whitened metric — and BENCHMARK against their released code
on the same Unsloth Qwen3-8B Q2_K checkpoint (CPU + 1-GPU eval, not a
sweep block). "First to improve shipped GGUFs in-format" is dead as a
claim; "cheapest, and whitened" is the live one.

### T2. ReQuant — frozen-grid discrete refinement as generic PTQ post-processing — CONFIRMED
**arXiv 2608.07019 (2026-08-07), Ma/Wang/Wang/Li/Zhang/Yan/Han/Yang (PKU + CUHK-SZ + ZTE per cn-labs lane; affiliation not on abstract page)**
Verified from abstract: backprop-free post-processing that "keeps
quantized weights improvable after an executable quantized model has
been produced, while preserving the quantized format"; strictly
loss-reducing coordinate updates on the frozen grid; PTQ-initializer
agnostic. Per the cn-labs full read: closed-form move scoring
dL = −dq·g + dq²·H_jj (activation-reconstruction objective with
diagonal Hessian), K=2 neighborhood, rank-one gradient maintenance;
Llama-3 8B/70B, Qwen3-14B/235B at W4A16→W2A4. No GGUF/K-quant, no
imatrix/whitened metric, no KLD evaluation.
**Impact:** together with GSQ, the generic "post-export code
re-optimization" frame is now published TWICE in different optimizer
families (deterministic coordinate descent; stochastic relaxation).
gguf-refine's contribution statement must be: (1) K-quant codec
instantiation (two-level sub-block scale structure), (2)
imatrix-whitened objective vs their plain reconstruction, (3)
paired-KLD evidence at production scale, (4) one-shot cost. Their
diagonal-Hessian move scoring is directly comparable to our error
feedback on cached Grams — a paper-math comparison, zero GPU.

### T3. OBD-LLM — bi-directional (Kronecker two-sided) whitening for LLM low-rank decomposition — CONFIRMED (core), residual application LANE-REPORTED
**arXiv 2604.00821 (2026-04-01), Yuhang Li, Donghyun Lee, Ruokai Yin, Priyadarshini Panda — the Yale KronQ group**
Verified from abstract: "a bi-directional whitening on the weight
matrix" using "second-order Hessian information" via "a rigorous
Kronecker-factorization," input AND output side, "much better
decomposition results compared to input only method"; ~20-40% over

post-GPTQ residual decomposition with 10% diagonal dampening and

the abstract; read the full PDF before the related-work section is
frozen.
**Impact:** our "two-sided (grad × activation) beats one-sided" (M3,
+~1% PPL, capture 0.76 vs 0.60) is no longer a novel metric claim —
it published four months before our measurement, and the same group's
KronQ (2607.07964, COLM 2026) confirms it for rounding/rotation.
Reframe as: *independent confirmation of the two-sided metric; first
instantiation in GGUF K-quant residual correction with byte-fair
interpolation controls.* Bonus, not just damage: their reported
dampening recipe is a direct test against our E24 "single-pass

### T4. TwinQuant — joint learnable subspace decomposition, both branches 4-bit, fused dual-component kernel — CONFIRMED
**arXiv 2606.01556 (2026-06-01), ICML 2026, HKUST + SYSU (Wang, Guo, Chen et al.)**

argues the low-rank component must ALSO be stored/computed in low
precision (their Obs. 2-3: FP16 rank-256 branch = ~50% of the 4-bit

**Three distinct hits:**
(a) **E16 phrasing** — NOT the "BF16 subspace + NVFP4 residual"
composition the serving lane claimed, and not post-hoc correction of
an NVFP4 base; but it IS an LLM-scale quant+low-rank joint scheme on
Blackwell-era formats. E16's claim must be narrowed to: *post-hoc
whitened low-rank residual correction of an unmodified NVFP4 base,
servable via the stock adapter path* — which remains unpublished
across all eight lanes (see quiet-lane section).
(b) **Fusion trilogy** — the serving lane's "fusion lane is QUIET"
verdict is punctured by TwinQuant's own fused dual-branch epilogue
kernel. Our unclaimed residue: batch-1 CUDA decode, dequant+adapter
epilogue on K-quant/GGUF, and the register-neutral occupancy law.
Related-work must cite their kernel.
(c) **Free support** — their Figure 1 (LLaMA3-8B spectra flat beyond
r≈256) is the published unwhitened cousin of our capture-vs-width
observation; cite it, then present our whitened c·r/d measurement as

Also note for MANIFOLD-CANDIDATES: this is a Stiefel-manifold joint
optimization that WINS in print — a counterexample at the margin to

### T4b. SVDQuant — shipping NVFP4-base + low-rank-branch composition, diffusion-only — CONFIRMED (follow-up sweep)
**arXiv 2411.05007 (v1 2024-11, v4 2025-11-08), ICLR 2025 Spotlight, MIT HAN Lab; engine: github.com/nunchaku-ai/nunchaku**

NVFP4-base + rank-32 low-rank system with fused Blackwell kernels.
**What survives (the whole measured result):** composition ORDER —
SVDQuant does plain unweighted SVD of the (smoothed) weight BEFORE
quantization, as outlier absorption, and the residual gets quantized;
E16 fits a whitened low-rank correction to the quantization error of
a frozen NVFP4 base. Plain SVD vs our full-cov/two-sided whitening;
no LLM instance; no GGUF/llama.cpp/stock-adapter path. Defensive
evidence the narrowed claim holds: DeepCompressor's LLM lane ships
QoQ/GPTQ/AWQ/SmoothQuant WITHOUT the low-rank branch, and its issue
#88 explicitly asking for SVDQuant-on-LLMs (hypothesizing 325σ LLM
activation outliers vs 28σ diffusion, fusion inefficiency at LLM
scale, global-SVD vs per-group conflict) has sat unanswered since
2025-06 — the lab that built the engine has not crossed to LLMs in
~14 months. Cite the repo scope split as evidence the LLM extension
is non-trivial.
**Paper change (required, before submission):** rewrite E16's novelty
sentence to *first LLM instance of an NVFP4-base + low-rank
composition, fitted as a whitened residual correction (LQER/QERA
form) rather than SVDQuant's pre-quantization plain-SVD outlier
absorption*, citing 2411.05007 + Nunchaku as the diffusion precedent.
Cheap differentiator from cached spectra: whitened-error capture of
rank-32 plain SVD of W (their order) vs our whitened rank-32 of the
NVFP4 residual — quantifies why residual-correction order wins for
LLMs and preempts the reviewer question. Two side notes: SVDQuant's
smoothing step is AWQ-family scale equalization — the lever we
measured anti-helpful on K-quants; and the Nunchaku fusion trick is
must-cite prior art for the fused-epilogue kernel section alongside
TwinQuant's dual-branch kernel (T4b joins T4(b)'s citation list).

## CONTEXT (citation-grade, one line each)

- **KronQ** (2607.07964, COLM 2026, Yale; v2 2026-08-08): two-sided Kronecker metric — K-FAC H≈HX⊗HG with HG the TRUE-Fisher output-gradient covariance (labels sampled from the model's own output dist, one backward pass) — used for (1) Bidirectional Incoherence Processing (input AND output-side rotation; output-only degrades severely, both sides needed; CVin 0.36/CVout 0.34) and (2) trace-based bit allocation; 2-bit LLaMA-3-70B ppl 7.93 where GPTQ/GPTAQ diverge. Cite with OBD-LLM as the metric's other instantiations. Two load-bearing details from the follow-up read: their Prop 1 shows HG cancels algebraically in the column-wise rounding update — they deploy the noisy gradient factor only where it cancels or merely ranks, which is published theoretical support for our "estimator quality gates metric sophistication" lesson and for why single-pass K-FAC whitening failed (E24/M1); and the claim split to write down is *their* two-sided ROTATION for rounding vs *our* two-sided whitened METRIC for residual fitting. Pre-GPU: compute µ-incoherence of cached gradient Grams (attn_q/k/v/o) and check their Fig-1 coherence pattern on Qwen3.5-27B.
- **MixQuant** (2607.23047): strongest recent mixed-precision allocator; never runs the vs-low-rank-at-equal-bytes comparison — evidence E27's control methodology is still uniquely ours.
- **Provable PTQ theory** (2508.04853): OPTQ/Qronos error-feedback bounds — check transfer to the K-quant grid for a free gguf-refine convergence guarantee.
