## Addendum 2026-10-22 — pre-final numbers audit (commit de6ee1a)

A trace of every quantitative claim in `paper/final/sections/` to its results
file. What it changed:

- **Whole-test-set signs (§4.3).** The corrected-minus-control margin was
  printed as t = −5.50 (and −4.96 on chunks 101–580), the sign of
  control-minus-corrected. It now reads +0.0051, t = +5.50 and +4.96, the
  table's A − B convention. Item 6 above is restated in that convention.
- **Held-out ratio (abstract, §7.5) — W8 now fully scoped.** The 10-16
  response (item 1) replaced "about half as much" with the ladder and
  recovery ratios but left the abstract and §7.5 saying the held-out gain is
  smaller, which §5.3 contradicts on the head-to-head files. Both now say:
  0.25 to 0.96 of the wikitext gain in the ladder and recovery comparisons,
  and 0.84 to 1.05 on the head-to-head files, which covers every model the
  referee's W8 named (0.8B, 8B, Qwen3.6-27B, Qwen3.8-27B).
- **Likelihood at both classes (abstract).** The trained files are further
  from BF16 at both size classes; their higher likelihood of the text is
  resolved at the IQ3 class only (t = −2.49; IQ2 t = −1.64). The abstract
  now says so.
- **Independence (§7.1).** The limitation calling the wikitext 27B rows a
  non-independent reproduction now applies to the 100-chunk comparison
  only; chunks 101–580 of A-full are an independent sample.
- **Build named (§4.4, §4.6).** Both subsections now say their rows predate
  the normalization fix; §4.4 gives the correction's increment as 0.0090 on
  that build against 0.0108 on b10865.
- **Cross-build agreement (§4.3).** 0.87 for the pair with Q3_K_S and 0.95
  for the pair with the control, with the chunk splits, now recorded in
  `experiments/36-submission/RESULTS-A.md`, "Chunk splits and cross-build
  agreement".
- **Anonymity (commit 500f617).** "A public version-control history", "the
  previous version", "this revision" and "a reader of the repository" told a
  double-blind reviewer that an earlier public copy exists. They now read "an
  earlier draft", "the final round" and "the supplementary material"; the
  history is promised for the de-anonymized version.
- Smaller: §4.7 row 4 states the held-out ordering; §5.1 calls the
  extraction time wall time on the CPU, not CPU-minutes; §5.4 labels the
  three increments by base; §6.1 and §6.2 cite the right sources.

wikitext-2 test set. Amendment A-full (registered in
