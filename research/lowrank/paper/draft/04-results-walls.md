# 4. Results II: the measured walls

This section is an anatomy of negative results. Each wall is named, measured, and given a mechanism; several were the campaign's most statistically secure findings [source: research/lowrank/review/adversarial-stats.md §F15].

## 4.1 Wall zero: neither W nor its residual is low-rank (E01/E03)

The spectrum census [F2] over all 197 tensors of Qwen3-0.6B: capturing 95% of Frobenius energy needs 50-80% of full rank on every projection type; the compression ratio at that rank is ≈1.0 (best: attn_q at 1.09x; V and FFN below 1.0 — factorization *costs* memory there). The stable-rank column (50-200 of 1024) explains why the "LLMs are low-rank" intuition persists: the top of the spectrum is concentrated, but 95% of energy lives deep in a long fat tail [source: research/lowrank/experiments/01-svd-spectrum/README.md]. The quantization residual is worse: r90 ≈ 62% of full rank on average — *less* spectral structure than W itself, behaving as near-isotropic round-off noise, exactly as the Marchenko-Pastur analysis in LQER (arXiv:2402.02446) predicts, and as RILQ (arXiv:2412.01129) independently diagnosed for the 2-bit regime: the error is inherently high-rank and layer-local correction saturates *(RILQ citation added 2026-08-04 — the published version of this wall was uncited; round-2 paper review C1)* — and an unweighted rank-128 F16 correction of a Q2_K base recovers 26-44% of error energy while costing 5.26 bpw, already more than Q4_K [F3] [source: research/lowrank/experiments/03-residual-rank/README.md]. Everything that follows rides on activation-weighted geometry; the unweighted framing is dead at both ends.

## 4.2 Capture versus width: from a two-point observation to a fitted form (E33)

Diagonal-whitened rank-64 capture of the Q2_K residual: 0.37 at d = 1024 (0.6B), 0.07 at d = 5120 (27B) — a fivefold fall for a fivefold width growth, at fixed rank [F8]. This is consistent with three published mechanisms: a whitened outlier head of near-constant cardinality (massive-activation/super-weight channels number ~6-100 regardless of width; arXiv:2208.07339, arXiv:2402.17762, arXiv:2411.07191) diluted by bulk energy growing with d; CALDERA's (1−k/2n)² bound and its own tables showing fixed-rank benefit collapsing 7B→70B (arXiv:2405.18886); and the intrinsic-LoRA lower bound forcing rank to scale near-linearly with width (arXiv:2606.01412). The two points were the observation; the regression (E33, 2026-09-11) is the measurement. Recomputing whitened spectra with exact totals for 1,146 tensors × 10 ranks × 3 whiteners on four models — Qwen3-0.6B and Qwen3.5-0.8B at d = 1024, heretic-27B and Qwen3.8-27B at d = 5120, all from BF16/Q8-provenance pairs with the extractor's own whitening (the pipeline reproduces §4.2's 0.37 and 0.07 and matches 1,620 logged captures per tensor to ≤0.01) — the linear rule capture = c·(r/d) is the *worst* of six candidate forms under every whitener (ΔAIC ≈ +12,000 diagonal, +24,000 full-Gram; held-out 27B prediction off 1.5–3.3×). Returns to rank are sublinear and width enters more strongly than rank: the best pooled form is capture ≈ a·r^{b_r}·d^{b_d} with b_r = 0.61 [0.60, 0.61], b_d = −0.67 [−0.70, −0.64] (diagonal) and b_r = 0.44 [0.44, 0.45], b_d = −0.59 [−0.61, −0.57] (full-Gram); r and d do not enter only through r/d (b_r + b_d = −0.06 [−0.09, −0.04] and −0.15 [−0.16, −0.13]). At fixed rank and tensor kind, diagonal-whitened capture falls as d^{−0.8…−1.1} across the d_model-input kinds (ffn_gate −1.13 [−1.17, −1.09]) and full-Gram capture as d^{−0.5…−0.8} — whitening by the full Gram halves the width penalty. The "constant head + isotropic bulk" form fits h = 0: no width-invariant outlier-head fraction is visible in these spectra, so the constant-cardinality-head mechanism above is *not* supported by this regression and needs a direct test. The prefactor is not a constant, and not even a per-whitener constant — but the variable is the *base recipe*, not the model: at diagonal r64, c = capture·d/r is 5.9 (0.6B), 5.2 (0.8B) and 5.9 (heretic-27B — the two-point value reproduces), and on Unsloth's shipped dynamic quant of Qwen3.8-27B it reads 10.0 (full-Gram 9.5 / 9.3 / 22.7 / 11.5). Re-quantizing Qwen3.8 from BF16 with exactly heretic's recipe (one Q2_K step, our whitening imatrix, same 11.0 GB byte class) collapses that difference entirely: heretic and Qwen3.8 are indistinguishable at every kind, rank and whitener (diag r64 0.074 vs 0.073, fc r64 0.284 vs 0.290; 0 of 40 per-kind contrasts exclude zero; identical rank exponents), so two qwen35 models of the same width sit on one curve and the shipped dynamic quant sits on another — dynamic bit allocation plus its own calibration inflates diagonal-visible residual structure by ~70 % at r64 and halves full-Gram-visible structure. With the fixed-recipe lane as primary, the pooled exponents tighten (diag b_r = 0.68 [0.67, 0.68], b_d = −0.76 [−0.79, −0.73]; full-Gram b_r = 0.37 [0.36, 0.37], b_d = −0.52 [−0.54, −0.50]) and holding out either 27B model now predicts it to 0.9–1.1× [source: research/lowrank/experiments/33-t110-capture-width/REPORT.md, fit_summary.txt, addendum_summary.txt]. Scope, stated plainly: at fixed kind the across-model width axis is one step (1024 → 5120), the two 27B models share d, the CIs are tensor-resampling CIs (1,000 cluster bootstraps) and not model-resampling CIs, and the prefactor is a property of (whitener, base recipe, width) — a third width (an 8B at d = 4096, or a ~70B) is required before any exponent is quoted as a law [source: research/lowrank/RESULTS_ROLLUP.md corrections 5; review/adversarial-stats.md §F6]. *(Section rewritten 2026-09-11 from the E33 regression; the T1.10 queue item is closed.)* What is safely established is the direction and its design consequence: at production widths, deployable ranks are a small fraction of dimension, and correction economics degrade faster than byte economics improve. The published counterpoint sharpens the mechanism: SVDQuant (arXiv:2411.05007) makes rank-32 work by *engineering* concentration — smoothing manufactures the outlier spike its low-rank branch then eats — which is exactly the structure a generic quantization residual lacks; without a manufactured spike, tiny rank buys r/d and no more *(contrast added 2026-08-04 per round-2 paper review C1)*.

## 4.3 The correction-versus-ladder audit at three dense scales

The decisive comparison is not against round-to-nearest but against llama.cpp's own imatrix-weighted ladder, with the interpolation control drawn [F9].

**27B: parity, not victory — re-derived BF16-pure with the control built (E27).** The whole 27B story was rebuilt under clean provenance: every base one quantization step from the BF16 reference, a fresh 48-chunk BF16 imatrix+Gram capture, BF16 truth logits (provenance-verified), and paired per-chunk statistics throughout [source: research/lowrank/experiments/27-bf16-rederivation/README.md]. The geometry-aware flagship — MIXED (FFN at Q3_K where r/d dooms correction, Q2_K elsewhere) + full-covariance r128-Q8 correction on non-FFN (capture 0.42, reproduced exactly) — reaches KLD 0.0863 ± 0.0054 at 12.50 GB total versus Q3_K_S's 0.0845 ± 0.0044 at 12.37 GB: a paired KLD *tie* (t = 0.6, 10/20 chunks), top-1 tied (t = 1.0), with a real paired PPL edge (t = −2.5). The legacy 1.0-sigma KLD "margin" did not survive — its sign flipped under clean provenance, confirming it as winner's-curse-plus-provenance artifact [review/adversarial-stats.md §F1, §F10]. The interpolation control, finally built at 27B (a pure-quant mixed-type base at the flagship's exact byte point, −0.016% bytes: Q3_K_S + attn_k/v/output→Q4_K + partial attn_qkv promotion), *matches-or-beats* the corrected config — KLD 0.0723 ± 0.0026 versus 0.0863 (paired t = 1.9 in the control's favor, 16/20 chunks; PPL and top-1 tied) — while beating Q3_K_S on all three metrics (|t| ≥ 2.5). So at 27B, as at every other scale, the ladder's interpolation line is unbeaten: the corrected flagship sits on it for PPL/top-1 and below it for KLD. The correction's increment over its own base is nonetheless real and paired-resolved (−0.0081 KLD, t = −5.9; +0.65 pt top-1, t = +2.5) — the machinery works, but base bits spend the same 336 MB better here. Three further clean-provenance facts. First, MIXED *bare* loses to Q3_K_S paired (KLD t = +3.6): the base mix alone does not tie the rung — it is the correction increment that closes the gap to parity, which sharpens rather than weakens the increment-is-real point *(clause added 2026-08-04 per round-2 paper review C4)*. Second, IQ3_XS keeps the byte-class KLD crown from below (0.0666 ± 0.0025; beats Q3_K_S paired t = −2.8). Third, every rebuilt rung scores 0.002-0.003 KLD better than its legacy Q6-requant sibling — a *rebuild tax (provenance + calibration)*: the rebuild changed the imatrix along with the provenance, and the one rung that was already BF16-single-step (Q4_K_M) improved by the same ~0.0025, so the attribution to provenance alone does not hold as stated; the isolating requant-with-new-imatrix run is queued *(attribution corrected 2026-08-04 per round-2 experiments review F2)*. Statistical honesty on the control verdict: the KLD t = +1.9 (n = 20) is below this paper's own 2σ bar over a heavy-tailed per-chunk distribution; the sign test on the recorded 16/20 chunk wins resolves at p ≈ 0.012 and dropping the single outlier chunk gives t ≈ +3.0, so the direction is very likely real, but "matches-or-beats" is carried on the sign/robustness evidence, with the 40-chunk rerun queued (ABLATION-PLAN T1.16) — and the control is properly described as a *designed* same-byte mixed-quant control (built once, bytes predicted a priori, using our own measured kind-sensitivities), a product of the same research program minus the adapter *(both clauses added 2026-08-04 per round-2 experiments review F3)*. Two caveats carried from the record: these are 20-chunk KLD rows, run to settle orderings under §2.5's disclosed waiver; and the "controls beat crowns at every scale" echo is an *inference* at 0.6-0.8B, where the interpolation control was never physically built (ABLATION-PLAN T1.6) *(per round-2 experiments review F5)*. The surviving finding, stated with the control in front: *a corrected 2-bit-geometry base reaches the K-quant ladder at 27B (statistical parity with Q3_K_S), and does not exceed the ladder's own interpolation at equal bytes* [F4 plots the byte/VRAM-versus-quality Pareto with the ladder, the control, and the corrected configs — the mission-axis figure; reference added 2026-08-04 per round-2 paper review §1.3].

**0.6B and 0.8B: wins-from-below.** At 0.6B the composed crown (alternation x2 + full covariance + Q8 factors, 382 MB) reaches PPL 25.27 / KLD 0.209 versus the rung below (Q3_K_M, 347 MB, 26.06/0.238 — the KLD margin ~5 sigma on-distribution, surviving Bonferroni over the configs tried) [source: research/lowrank/RESULTS_ROLLUP.md; review/adversarial-stats.md §F7]. At 0.8B, with BF16-truth scoring, fc-r128q8 ties Q3_K_M and one alternation round beats it at +9% bytes (KLD 0.147 vs 0.167, ~3 sigma estimated; PPL only 1.6 sigma) [source: research/lowrank/experiments/22-qwen35-08b/README.md]. But at both scales the comparison rung was chosen from below: the ladder's own interpolation at the crown's byte point (≈0.118 at 382 MB; ≈0.080 at 523 MB) beats the crown on both metrics, and Q4_K_M at +4% bytes is 3-4x better on KLD. No mixed-quant interpolation control was built at small scale — the control we *did* build at 27B, and which §4.5 shows demolishing the MoE correction. The honest statement, applied uniformly: the method transfers across architectures and creates points non-dominated by the *discrete* ladder inside the one ~50 MB between-rungs window; the Q4 tier rules above it, everywhere, and the ladder's interpolation line is unbeaten. E22's real finding is the transfer itself — the machinery ran unchanged on a second architecture in ~35 minutes on our patched toolchain [source: research/lowrank/experiments/22-qwen35-08b/README.md].

**NVFP4 as a carrier, ground-up at 0.8B (E16 rung 1 / E34).** The first whitened low-rank correction on a frozen NVFP4 LLM base is real — 40/40 chunks, KLD 0.207 → 0.104, top-1 +6.2 points — and loses: the 603 MB composite trails every bare rung at or below its bytes (Q4_K_S at 519 MB, 0.047; Q5_K_S at 578 MB, 0.015), the fourth base family under the equal-byte law of §4.3b. Under llama.cpp's quantizer, which has no NVFP4-specific importance path, NVFP4 is the *worst* 4-bit carrier on this hybrid-GDN architecture (bare 0.207 against Q4_0's 0.088 at equal bytes), and its marginally higher residual capture (0.736 versus Q3_K_M's 0.721) does not buy a larger increment — Q3_K_M recovers 70% of its damage to NVFP4's 50%. Capture is a statement about what the factors can see, not about where the composition lands [source: research/lowrank/experiments/34-e16-nvfp4-08b/README.md; JOURNAL 2026-09-14].

**The tax asterisk on all of the above.** Every corrected configuration pays a stock-llama.cpp decode tax of 20-40% that the bare rungs do not (§5); "competes with the rung" and "stock llama.cpp" are never true simultaneously today. On-distribution evaluation is the second asterisk: calibration and evaluation share wikitext-2 (train/test split only), and §4.7 shows the method is strongly distribution-sensitive; held-out corpora and task evaluations are queued (§6).

## 4.3b The equal-byte equivalence class: mechanism doesn't matter, allocation does (E28/E29)

A pre-registered afternoon of paired experiments at 27B asked whether *where* the low-rank bytes act changes what they buy. Three mechanisms were built at matched bytes against the same flagship: the post-hoc whitened correction (§2.2, the flagship itself), a pre-quant whitened carve-out in the SRR/preserve-then-quantize order (arXiv:2602.02001) — carve C = B·A off the BF16 weights, quantize the deflated remainder with the identical mixed recipe, serve C as the adapter — and shared-basis variants sharing one A factor across input-sharing tensor groups (the GlowQ shape, arXiv:2603.25385). Their *surrogate* captures differ wildly: 40% of the residual, 76% of the whitened weight matrix at k=128 (E29's finding that E01's raw-SVD full-rank null does not transfer to the whitened metric at 27B), +12.2% union capture for the shared basis at parity rank. Their measured quality is indistinguishable: SRR-carve vs flagship KLD t = +0.99, shared-A parity vs flagship t = −0.91, all |t| < 2 on KLD/NLL/top-1 at 100 paired rows each [source: research/lowrank/JOURNAL.md, E28b/E28c/E29 2026-08-11]. The capture surrogate overstating a lever is the campaign's sixth surrogate-vs-outcome divergence; SchurQuant's published observation — "[l]ayer-wise reconstruction is a loose surrogate for the final language-model loss" (arXiv:2608.15567 §1; their abstract adds that at higher precision "tighter reconstruction does not consistently improve end-model metrics") — independently corroborates the pattern. Meanwhile the mixed-TYPE control beats every class member (t ≥ +2.3) and the uniform rung sits below (flagship over Q3_K_S, t = −2.85). The synthesis, stated as this paper's claim: **at 27B, at equal bytes, {post-hoc correction, pre-quant carve, shared-basis} form an equivalence class — the byte budget and its type allocation, not the low-rank mechanism, set quality.** Scope stated per the nearest published duel (AWSRC, arXiv:2608.23144, whose repair-codec winner flips between PPL and KL at 3B): *among low-rank mechanisms, at 27B, on K-quant grids, at equal bytes*. The codec-class membership question is measured, not open: E31's pre-registered capture duel on the cached whitened spectra (18 tensors × 3 seeds) finds AWSRC-class seeded bases capture 0.035 of dominant whitened energy at byte parity versus 0.983 for learned factors — exactly 1.00× the random-subspace expectation, i.e. statistically indistinguishable from random bases in whitened geometry at 27B — so seeded repair sits strictly *below* the equivalence class on head capture and ties only on the isotropic tail (E03); their 3B win is attributed, as a registered testable hypothesis, to low-bit coefficient packing plus an unwhitened low-rank baseline [source: research/lowrank/experiments/31-seeded-basis-duel/PREREG.md]. The escaping axis is precisely the one RCO (arXiv:2605.00649) optimizes — per-tensor type allocation under exact byte budgets, calibration-KL-objective, at 8B — and their paper contains no mechanism-class comparison: our measurement and their optimizer are complementary halves of one statement — allocation is the only lever that moves at equal bytes, and it can be optimized exactly. One deployable residue survives the null: the shared-A basis at r128 ties the flagship's quality at −16.6% adapter bytes (280.8 vs 336.5 MB, KLD t = −1.48), a real VRAM saving under the loader-alias patch class [source: research/lowrank/JOURNAL.md, E28c]. *(Section added 2026-09-09; measurements 2026-08-11, pre-registered rules fired as recorded. Family placements: research/lowrank/paper/theory-L6-family-subsumption.md, Claim 5.)*

## 4.3c Trained versus one-shot frozen-grid refinement: the GSQ-RCO head-to-head at 27B (E32 / T1.17, third family)

The frozen-grid family of §2/§7 now has a published, artifact-level competitor on consumer formats: IST-DASLab's GSQ-RCO (arXiv:2604.18556 + 2605.00649) ships refined GGUF checkpoints of Qwen3.8-27B — Gumbel-Softmax re-selection of the discrete codes on shipped K/IQ grids, with per-tensor type allocation optimized under an exact byte budget: per their card, every tensor is GSQ-quantized at every candidate GGUF type into a database, RCO selects one type per tensor under the budget, and the selections are stitched into a standard GGUF. The calibration corpus of the released files is not documented, and the public GSQ repository contains no GGUF path at all (its quantizers train the group scales as free floats; theory-L6 addendum 2026-09-11), so the comparison is scoped to the *released artifacts*, not to a mechanism ablation. Per the family placement in theory-L6 (Claim 2), this is *search-and-granularity*, not surrogate-versus-outcome: at layer granularity GSQ optimizes the same quadratic our closed-form pass solves, by stochastic relaxation instead of one exact whitened solve. The head-to-head was pre-registered (experiments/32-t117-gsq-head-to-head/README.md) on the same shipped Unsloth Dynamic baselines their model card compares against, graded on 40 chunks of wikitext-2 test against BF16 truth logits regenerated for this family, with a fresh 48-chunk BF16 imatrix+Gram capture (the E27 recipe unchanged on a hybrid Gated-DeltaNet architecture — the third architecture family the machinery has run on) [source: research/lowrank/experiments/32-t117-gsq-head-to-head/results-40ch.txt, paired-wikitext-40ch.txt].

**The KLD half is won, at both rungs, by the one-shot pass.** Our r128-Q8 whitened correction on the bare Unsloth IQ2_S base reaches KLD 0.1191 ± 0.0027 (top-1 85.8 %) at 8.37 + 0.90 GB against GSQ-RCO IQ2_XS's 0.2028 ± 0.0044 (81.4 %) at 8.42 GB — paired ΔKLD −0.0837 ± 0.0073, t = −11.5, 40/40 chunks, top-1 +4.4 pt (t = +10.9). At the IQ3 rung — their "task-lossless" operating point — ours reaches 0.0353 ± 0.0011 against 0.0525 ± 0.0012: ΔKLD −0.0173 ± 0.0018, t = −9.5, 38/40 chunks, top-1 +1.6 pt (t = +4.7). The sharper fact sits one row up: on this instrument GSQ-RCO lands *below its own untrained baseline* — the bare Unsloth quant at the same byte class beats the trained refinement at both rungs (IQ2: +0.0715 KLD, t = +11.7, 0/40 chunks in GSQ's favor; IQ3: +0.0152, t = +8.6, 1/40). The correction increment over its own bare base is real at all three rungs measured (IQ2_S −0.0122, t = −6.5, 39/40; Q2_K_XL −0.0055, t = −7.0, 34/40; IQ3_S −0.0021, t = −5.9, 36/40), with the top-1 increment real at the two 2-bit rungs (+0.7 pt, t ≈ +3.8) and tied at IQ3_S (t = +0.5). **The equal-byte law survives its third family.** Byte-fair against the ladder rather than the competitor, the result is §4.3's again: the bare Unsloth Q2_K_XL at 9.83 GB beats our corrected IQ2_S-plus-adapter at 9.27 GB by 0.0337 KLD (t = +11.6, 0/40). ## 4.4 The recovery curve and the IQ1 asymptote

Held to a single estimator (full covariance), the fraction of the base's KLD damage recovered by correction rises monotonically as the base approaches its cliff: 20% at Q2_K, 28% at IQ2_XS, 41% at IQ1_S [F7] [source: research/lowrank/RESULTS_ROLLUP.md corrections 6; review/adversarial-stats.md §F5]. (The steeper 7.5%→18%→41% curve in early notes mixed diagonal and full-covariance points and is retired. A residual confound the metric fix does not cure, disclosed per the ablation audit: even the fc-consistent curve varies adapter budget — 866/866/799 MB — and rank mix across its three points, so no published point holds the correction recipe fixed while varying only the base; the recipe-held-fixed rebuild is ABLATION-PLAN T1.9 *— caveat added 2026-08-04 per round-2 paper review R5*.) The curve's qualitative content matches ResComp's inverse-bit-width claim (arXiv:2604.07955) and EoRA's biggest-at-2-3-bit gains: correction pays most exactly where pure quantization collapses.

The asymptote test takes this to its limit — the founding pure-factorization hypothesis in its last servable form *(anonymization fix 2026-08-04 per round-2 paper review R8)*: the thinnest carrier llama.cpp has (IQ1_S, 1.56 bpw, 7.44 GB) with factors doing maximum representational work. Scaling the corrector to r512 non-FFN / r256 FFN (2.52 GB of Q8 factors, BF16-pure residuals) lifts recovery to 52% — and still loses to bare Q2_K at near-equal total bytes by ~3x KLD (10.50 PPL/0.431 at 9.96 GB versus 7.891/0.153 at 10.86 GB). The exchange rate is brutal and worsening: the adapter's second and third gigabytes bought ~0.10 KLD in *total* (0.527→0.431 over +1.72 GB, i.e. ~0.056 per GB) where a gigabyte of carrier bits buys ~0.28 — a ~5× contrast *(unit fixed 2026-08-04: the previous "~0.10 KLD per GB" flattered the adapter ~2× and understated the contrast as ~3×; round-2 paper review M2)* [source: research/lowrank/experiments/15-iq1-carrier/README.md]. *Factors cannot substitute for carrier bits.* The constructive corollary is the campaign's design rule (§4.8); the destructive one is the epitaph of pure factorization at calibration-only budgets, agreeing with the published price list for real factorization ratios (50B+ retraining tokens: Sheared-LLaMA arXiv:2310.06694, Minitron arXiv:2407.14679).

The corrected IQ1_S point itself (8.24 GB file, KLD 0.891→0.527, top-1 +8.5 points, 16.5 sigma) is the one place the ladder offers no measured rival rung — with the review's caveats attached: the equal-byte rivals IQ1_M and IQ2_XXS were never built, and 8.24 GB is file bytes, projecting to ~10.3-10.5 GB VRAM on the measured adapter-overhead pattern [source: research/lowrank/review/adversarial-claims.md §F4].

## 4.5 The MoE wall: the pooled expert residual is nearly isotropic — no shared subspace to amortize (E23)

*(Title reworded 2026-08-04: "mutually near-orthogonal" is dimensionally impossible for 256 subspaces in a 512-dim space; round-2 experiments review F9.)*

On Qwen3.6-35B-A3B (256 experts/stack, 8 routed; expert stacks are 91% of parameters), both correction variants are real and both lose the ladder decisively — with the interpolation control this time built in from the start [source: research/lowrank/experiments/23-moe/README.md]. Reference disclosure, per §2.5's targeted-reference rule: no BF16 of this model exists on our hardware, so every row (and the KLD scoring) is one quantization step from the strongest vendor artifact (UD-Q4_K_M, 22.1 GB) — uniform provenance within the table, with every verdict margin ≥2.7σ paired and the losing margins at 6.9-8.2σ, comfortably outside the reference-contamination scale measured at 27B *(disclosure added 2026-08-04 — §4.5 previously stated none of this while §2.5 promised BF16; round-2 paper review R4)*. Per-expert whitened correction (r32, Q8, pooled-Gram whitening; served on *stock* llama.cpp — build_lora_mm_id handles 3D adapter pairs natively, a fact we established by measurement) improves the pinned-Q2_K base at 4.6 paired sigma (18/20 chunks). But at its own byte point (14.5 GB) it loses to the *trivial* control — promote ffn_down_exps to Q4_K — by +0.0257 ± 0.0038 KLD at 6.9 sigma, 0/20 chunk wins; Q3_K_S at +0.7 GB is ~2x better again. Per byte spent, quant-type promotion buys ~4x more KLD reduction than rank-32 factors at this scale.

The sharper negative is structural. The shared-basis hypothesis — one output basis B per stack amortized across 256 experts, motivated by all experts reading/writing the same activation spaces — fails *at the capture level*: the shared basis captures 0.086-0.090 of whitened residual energy on up/gate stacks (gate 0.090, up 0.086) against a random-orthonormal floor of 0.0625, and 0.022 versus 0.0156 on down stacks *(per-stack numbers stated 2026-08-04 per round-2 paper review M5)*. Stated precisely: 256 subspaces cannot be literally mutually orthogonal in a 512-dim output space — what the shared-capture-at-the-floor measurement shows is that the *pooled* whitened residual spectrum is nearly isotropic, with no fat shared subspace to exploit at r = 32 *(wording corrected from "essentially mutually orthogonal" 2026-08-04 per round-2 experiments review F9)* [source: research/lowrank/experiments/23-moe/README.md]. Mechanistically the whole MoE wall is the r/d observation in miniature multiplied by expert count: each expert is a small 512x2048 matrix whose Q2→Q4 residual is nearly unstructured (per-expert capture 0.19-0.36 versus 0.7+ at dense small scale — measured under the pooled whitener, a disclosed approximation of each expert's conditional input covariance, so some of the "unstructured" reading may be whitener mismatch, mostly on down stacks), and thin-factor overhead is paid 256 times. Routes that could change the verdict — correcting the *dense* tensors of the MoE, or sub-2-bit expert bases where the ladder has no cheap promotion rung — are follow-ups, not rescues.

## 4.6 Estimator gates metric: the allocation and two-sided evidence

The campaign's fourth recurring lesson is that metric *quality* dominates rank, and estimator quality dominates metric sophistication — and, at the floor, that surrogate improvement stops predicting outcome at all. Positive direction: diagonal → full covariance at 0.6B was worth more than doubling rank (fc r64: PPL 27.58 / KLD 0.318 versus diag r64 31.29/0.420 and diag r128-Q8 28.46 — ~10 sigma and 2.6 sigma respectively), and the same-pipeline full-versus-diagonal ablation at 27B resolved at ~2 sigma (0.1460 → 0.1282 at r64) [source: research/lowrank/experiments/10-full-covariance/README.md]. Negative direction, four independent instances *(updated from three 2026-08-04 — E26 completes the set; round-2 paper review S4a)*: (i) greedy energy water-filling for rank allocation is actively harmful (34.17 versus uniform's 31.29 at equal bytes — it zeroes ffn_down, whose 25,600-dim inputs hold the massive-activation outliers the diagonal proxy cannot see); measured per-kind KL sensitivities fix the ordering (attn_k dominates at 0.0128 ΔKLD/MB, additivity verified: Σ isolated 0.352 ≈ joint 0.346), though the allocation-versus-uniform end-to-end margin at 27B is itself unresolved (0.5 sigma) and the 27B "measured" weights were transferred 0.6B priors — relabeled accordingly [source: research/lowrank/JOURNAL.md; review/adversarial-code.md §B6]. (ii) Gradient-side-only sensitivity anti-correlates with measured sensitivity (Spearman −0.29) exactly as input-only does; only the two-sided product flips positive (+0.39), and the leftover-correlation structure after diagonal whitening is *inverted* between the two sides per kind (grad-side headroom in attention K/V, input-side in attn_output/ffn_down) — either single side is a trap, and two-sided whitening is the only lane that collects both [source: research/lowrank/experiments/20-gradgram/REPORT.md]. Measured two-sided fc r64 at 0.6B: 27.27 versus 27.58 one-sided at equal bytes. (iii) The E24 Kronecker result (§3.2): the *exactly-solved* richer metric with a noisily-estimated factor is worse than the poorer metric estimated well, at 15 paired sigma. (iv) The E26 Lloyd null (§3.4), the cleanest instance of all: a 2.6% further descent of the *same* whitened surrogate — no estimator change, no metric change — produced exactly zero end-to-end effect (|t| ≤ 1.3 on all metrics) on a harness that resolves real effects at t = 27-47. The quotable form of the lesson: *below some floor, whitened-proxy descent stops predicting KL divergence* — an approximation of the metric is not a monotone approximation of the outcome, and neither is the metric itself.

## 4.6b The equalization null: importance belongs in the fit weights, not the columns (E26)

*(Section added 2026-08-04 — E26's second null was absent from the draft; round-2 paper review S4b.)*

The AWQ/SmoothQuant lineage moves importance into the weights: scale columns by an importance factor so the quantizer spends its dynamic range where activations are large. On an *imatrix-weighted* quantizer, we measured the full-strength version of this idea as the strongest-t negative in the paper. Method integrity first: on this architecture only *diagonal* gauge transformations fold exactly (every candidate rotation site sits behind an RMSNorm with learnable gamma), and restricting the scales to powers of two makes the fold a bit-exact gauge transformation — verified end-to-end on GPU, equalized BF16 versus stored BF16: KLD 0.000000, top-1 100.000% — a reusable methods nugget (pow2 folds are exact gauge transformations; the imatrix transforms exactly with the gauge). Requantizing the equalized model with the identical recipe at identical bytes is then strongly anti-helpful: PPL 33.09 → 41.73, paired t = +28..+60, equalization better on 0/40 KLD chunks; the quantizer's own imatrix-weighted objective degrades 6-68% per tensor (eq/bare error ratio 1.06-1.68 on folded tensors, exactly 1.000 on the untouched control) [source: research/lowrank/experiments/26-lloyd-gauge/README.md]. The defensible mechanism statement, per the round-2 review (F7): moving importance from the fit weights into the column scales is strongly harmful for Q2_K's shared-sub-scale format — the fold injects up to 32× dynamic range into 16-element sub-blocks that the shared 4-bit sub-scales cannot span, and flattens the informative weighting of the affine fit. Whether an "importance double-counting" component exists independently of the format interaction is *not* discriminated by this run (in exact arithmetic the weighted objective is gauge-invariant, and the equalized run counts importance once — in the columns); the discriminating controls (equalization without imatrix; an alpha sweep, where the AWQ lineage predicts an interior optimum) are queued as ABLATION-PLAN T1.13. As measured, the result is scoped to the pre-registered full-strength point (alpha = 1) — precisely where the equalization literature itself expects harm — on one format.

## 4.7 Task conditioning: the surviving form of the factorization dream

Fixing the target distribution collapses functional rank. Conditioning the correction's covariance on a code corpus buys +10.3% on-distribution perplexity at identical bytes (code-conditioned 2.746 versus wiki-conditioned 3.061 on code, ~8 sigma) at a steep off-distribution cost (34.90 versus 27.58 on wiki — a 27% swing), with asymmetric generalization: broad calibration transfers to narrow far better than the reverse [source: research/lowrank/experiments/17-task-conditioned/README.md]. This is simultaneously a product lane (per-workload adapters via llama.cpp's per-request LoRA API) and the sharpest internal caveat: the measured distribution sensitivity of the method (10-27%) exceeds every contested ladder margin in §4.3, which is why held-out-corpus evaluation is a gating repair, not an optional extension.

## 4.8 The co-design ordering rule

Assembled from §3.3 and the alternation arc, measured at two scales and paired-confirmed at a third: *joint shaping beats greedy sequential composition.* Final 0.6B co-design ranking: alternation x2 (25.27) > ProjQ-deflated one-shot re-round (25.94) > greedy re-round-then-correct (26.80), with the alternation lineage disclosed (LoftQ-descended; the novelty is the black-box llama-quantize grid, the whitened metric, and the termination certificate of §2.4) [source: research/lowrank/experiments/13-rerounder/README.md; review/adversarial-claims.md §F14]. Together with the geometry rule — correct where rank is a large fraction of width; spend base bits where it is not, validated constructively by MIXED at 27B and destructively by E15 and E23 — this is the paper's prescription to a practitioner: the order of operations is a measured result, not a convention.
