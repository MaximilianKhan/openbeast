# 6. Limitations and future work

## 6.1 Known limitations, from our own review

The campaign closed with an adversarial review of its statistics, claims, and code [source: research/lowrank/review/]; the surviving limitations are stated here as the paper's boundary conditions.

**Distribution.** Calibration and evaluation share wikitext-2 (train/test split only). The corrected configurations carry far more calibration-fitted capacity than the rungs they compete with, and our own E14-cond measures 10-27% corpus sensitivity — larger than every contested ladder margin. All "competes with the rung" statements are therefore on-distribution claims pending held-out confirmation; the near-baseline 27B endgame additionally sits inside KLD's silent zone (arXiv:2606.19558), where task evaluations are the only arbiter. Zero task evaluations were run in the campaign. The one held-out measurement we do have is adverse and is stated in §3.1: narrow (wikitext-only) calibration made the re-rounder *worse than bare* on a held-out code corpus (PPL 3.470 vs 3.083); a mixed-calibration variant recovered both corpora, but both evaluation corpora are then inside calibration coverage — the extrapolation question (a third, uncalibrated corpus) is untested and queued [source: research/lowrank/paper/DEPLOYABLE-WINS.md; ABLATION-PLAN T1.11]. *(Paragraph extended 2026-08-04: the measured anti-generalization counterexample was missing from this section — round-2 paper review C3a/R2.)*

**Controls.** The mixed-quant interpolation control exists at 27B and MoE scale but was never built at 0.6B/0.8B — the review's single most consequential gap, and the reason §4.3 claims wins-from-below rather than wins.

**References and precision.** Legacy 27B tables are Q6-referenced (measured contamination 0.0032 KLD — of the same order as two withdrawn orderings, including NVFP4+fc versus IQ3_XS); 20-chunk 27B PPL resolves almost nothing (±0.25-0.32). E27 closed the worst of this: the flagship triangle (MIXED+fc, Q3_K_S, interpolation control) is now BF16-pure and fully paired, and Q3_K_S was rebuilt one step from BF16 and truth-scored (0.0845 ± 0.0044) — the numbers §4.3 headlines. What remains open from the legacy tables: the *remaining* 27B levers (allocation-versus-uniform, re-rounding, alternation) are individually inside unpaired error bars pending paired tests, and the 27B VRAM columns are unmeasured. The 27B "full covariance" label means block-diagonal for ffn_down (§2.2), and the 27B allocation used transferred 0.6B kind priors. *(Rewritten 2026-08-04: "Q3_K_S was never BF16-rescored" was false since E27 and contradicted §4.3 — round-2 paper review S2 / coherence-audit P1.7.)*

**Evidence grade on alternation.** §4.8's ordering claim (never stack re-rounding under a corrector) is paired-resolved (E24, t = +17.6). The alternation-*rounds* claim itself is weaker than the prose suggests: it is confounded at every scale — the 0.6B arc is unpaired, the 0.8B round-1 continuation was run conditionally with no recorded error bars, and the 27B fc→fc+alt delta is ~1σ (ABLATION-PLAN §1.6). The paired third scale point supports the ordering, not the rounds curve. *(Added 2026-08-04 per round-2 paper review C3b.)*

**Speed.** Boost drift up to 9% between sessions; the 27B fused-kernel and allocator gains are direction-corroborated, magnitude-unresolved; kernel gates (Q8 size gates, MIN_ROWS) are tuned on exactly two rigs and one GPU.

**Code hazards, disclosed.** Cache directories originally carried no provenance fingerprint (silent-wrong on resume; fixed for the re-rounder codes-dir, open for spectra caches); --columns under Gram whitening ranks the wrong columns (latent; published column runs predate fc); naive MoE Gram accumulation would have been wrong (fixed properly in E23's capture) [source: research/lowrank/review/adversarial-code.md].

## 6.2b Reproducibility and artifacts

Every quality number in this paper derives from artifacts retained in the released experiment tree (`experiments/*/` READMEs with method, raw logs, per-chunk outputs, and reproduction commands; `data/` reference logits and Gram captures). Three components are *not* stock llama.cpp and are disclosed as such (§2.3): the Gram-capture imatrix extension (a local ~140-line env-gated C++ patch), the llama-gradmatrix tool, and the fused-kernel/allocator patch set (pinned at build SHA 0ef6e55ed with kill switches). The re-rounder and all extractors are standalone Python against gguf-py. Release plan: patches, tools, adapters, reference logits, and per-chunk logs ship with the paper; the private patches are the only build-time dependency. The pipeline's re-runnability is not asserted but measured: E26's reproduction gate rebuilt the E22 base 335/335 tensors byte-identical from the recorded recipe and re-derived the E13 re-rounded artifact byte-identical, and E24's provenance gate reproduced E22's published numbers exactly before any new comparison was run. Repo-path citations in this draft (`[source: research/lowrank/...]`) resolve inside that released tree. *(Section added 2026-08-04 per round-2 paper review R7.)*

## 6.3 Open lanes

**Codec coverage for the free lever.** The re-rounder speaks only Q2_K today; Q3_K/Q4_K codecs unlock the promoted tensors that muted the 27B result, and an NVFP4 codec (nearest-of-8 E2M1 on frozen FP8 scales) targets the format whose residual is provably covariance-structured (arXiv:2509.23202) — E16 measured it as the best-correcting 27B base (capture 0.52, −22% KLD). Target tool shape: `gguf-refine model.gguf`, K-quants through NVFP4 — shipping with a **mixed-calibration default and a held-out validation gate as requirements, not options**: §3.1's measured anti-generalization under narrow calibration makes an ungated version of this tool a quality hazard, not a convenience [source: research/lowrank/TODO.md 2026-08-04e; paper/DEPLOYABLE-WINS.md]. *(Gate clause added 2026-08-04 per round-2 paper review R2iii.)*

**Estimator repair for two-sided metrics.** E24's ranked follow-ups: T-damping/shrinkage-toward-I sweep (does the harm vanish smoothly?), larger gradient captures plus a delta-net adjoint to remove the gradient cut, and the 0.6B rerun that separates estimator noise from the single-pass K-FAC approximation itself. 27B gradient coverage (blocked >8192-dim path, hybrid-layer F32-norm coverage) is untested.

**Manifold survivors.** Of the differential-geometry candidate list [source: research/lowrank/prior-art/MANIFOLD-CANDIDATES.md], the measured survivors are ProjQ-style metric surgery (already positive: 25.94 one-shot) with its Bregman-damped proven-descent variant (arXiv:2507.09428), Riemannian refinement of the closed-form factors on fixed-rank manifolds, and the information-geometry reading of the whitening ladder; the Kronecker-global-metric lane is measured anti-helpful at current estimator quality (§3.2), and closed-form Fisher allocation with one-sided proxies is a confirmed trap.

**Product lanes.** Per-workload conditioned adapters (+10.3% on-task at equal bytes, per-request selectable through the existing LoRA API); the sparse-A fused-kernel lever (top correction directions live on 2-5% of input channels — participation-ratio audit [F10]); MoE dense-tensor correction and sub-2-bit expert carriers (the two unrescued routes E23 names); and the upstream bundle — re-rounder tool + Gram-capture imatrix extension, the allocator fix, the fusion trilogy — each independently useful, each review-scrubbed [source: research/lowrank/paper/DEPLOYABLE-WINS.md].

## 6.4 Closing statement

The honest ledger of this study: one lever that is free at fixed calibration breadth — paired-resolved at one scale (0.8B, with an independent paired replication by E26's reproduction gate), ~20σ-unpaired legacy at a second, unpaired at 27B — a correction paradigm that is real, composable, convergence-certified — and bounded by walls we can name, measure, and mostly explain; a kernel and allocator patch set that benefits users who will never load an adapter; and a protocol that would have prevented our own first week of overclaims. The quantization ladder that ships in llama.cpp is very close to its local optimum; the codes on its frozen grids are not. That asymmetry — the walls around the corrections, the freedom inside the grids — is the finding. *(Reworded 2026-08-04: "free everywhere" and "paired-resolved at two scales" both overstated the record — round-2 paper review R2/M7; "twenty hours" internal-voice removed per R8.)*
