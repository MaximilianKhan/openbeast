## Abstract

Consumer GPUs are compute-rich and memory-poor: an RTX 5090 pairs a datacenter-class processor with 32 GB of VRAM. We measure, on production llama.cpp artifacts, what post-training repair of aggressive quantization can and cannot buy under that constraint. The premise is a measured inversion: batch-1 decode is memory-bandwidth-bound, so a 27B model taken from 23.6 GB to 13.0 GB of VRAM decodes *faster* (61 to 99.7 tok/s), and accuracy is the only price of compression [source: research/lowrank/RESULTS_ROLLUP.md]. We contribute one lever that costs zero bytes and an audit of the levers that do not pay. The free lever is frozen-grid re-rounding: keep llama-quantize's per-block grids byte-for-byte and re-choose only the codes under a captured activation-covariance metric. At identical bytes it improves a Q2_K model by 15% perplexity and 34% KL divergence at 0.8B (30 to 45 paired standard errors) and by 13% KL divergence at 27B (t = −7.0, n = 100), and emits a standard GGUF that runs on every deployed llama.cpp build [source: research/lowrank/experiments/24-yaqa-lite/README.md; experiments/27-bf16-rederivation/results-100ch-paired.txt]. Its caveat is measured: calibrated on one corpus it transfers to nearby unseen text but is worse than the untouched model on a distant one, and mixed calibration repairs both [source: research/lowrank/paper/DEPLOYABLE-WINS.md]. The walls: weights and their quantization residuals are energy-full-rank; whitened low-rank correction is real at every scale, but its capture falls with width as a fitted power law rather than as r/d; and at 27B, with every artifact one quantization step from BF16 and paired per-chunk statistics, the corrected configuration beats the adjacent ladder rung (t = −2.85) and loses to a pure-quantization mixed-type control built at the same bytes (t = +2.62). Three different low-rank mechanisms are indistinguishable at equal bytes; only type allocation escapes. On a mixture-of-experts model the correction loses a trivial promotion control at 6.9 paired standard errors. Against the released GSQ-RCO checkpoints of a 27B model, a 13-minute one-shot correction wins KL divergence at both rungs on two corpora, yet the trained artifact wins long-context perplexity and a 112-unit agentic coding suite against its own untrained baseline (net +12 units, p = 0.012): short-context divergence from the reference does not rank artifacts across refinement families [source: research/lowrank/experiments/32-t117-gsq-head-to-head/]. We also report a fused-kernel series that removes most of the adapter decode tax (+79% at 0.6B, +2.7% at 27B), an allocator fix worth +19.6% decode to any CUDA llama.cpp user, and the measurement protocol — paired statistics, interpolation controls, single-step provenance, pre-registration — that an adversarial review of our own first claims forced on us.
